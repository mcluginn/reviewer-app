5-Minute Rapid Review

The essential theorems, derivative patterns, and algebraic mnemonics for rapid recall.

1. Continuity at \( x = c \)

3-Part Test

A function \( f \) is continuous at \( x = c \) if and only if ALL THREE hold:

  • 1. \( f(c) \) is defined (a real point exists on the graph).
  • 2. \( \lim_{x \to c} f(x) \) exists (\( \lim_{x \to c^-} f(x) = \lim_{x \to c^+} f(x) \)).
  • 3. \( \lim_{x \to c} f(x) = f(c) \) (the curve connects with the point).

2. Core Differentiation Rules

Formulas
Power Rule: \( \frac{d}{dx}[x^n] = n x^{n-1} \)
Product Rule: \( (uv)' = u'v + uv' \)
Quotient Rule: \( \left(\frac{u}{v}\right)' = \frac{v u' - u v'}{v^2} \)
Chain Rule: \( \frac{d}{dx}[f(g(x))] = f'(g(x))g'(x) \)

3. Trigonometric Derivatives

Sign Rule
💡 Mnemonic: Every "CO-" function has a NEGATIVE derivative!
\( (\sin x)' = \cos x \)
\( (\cos x)' = -\sin x \)
\( (\tan x)' = \sec^2 x \)
\( (\cot x)' = -\csc^2 x \)
\( (\sec x)' = \sec x \tan x \)
\( (\csc x)' = -\csc x \cot x \)

4. Motion Relationships

\( s \to v \to a \)
Position \( s(t) \)

Location coordinate on axis

Base
Velocity \( v(t) = s'(t) \)

Rate of change of position; \( v=0 \implies \) at rest

1st Deriv
Acceleration \( a(t) = v'(t) = s''(t) \)

Rate of change of velocity

2nd Deriv

5. Intermediate Value Theorem (IVT)

Existence
If \( f \) is continuous on closed interval \( [a, b] \) and \( N \) lies strictly between \( f(a) \) and \( f(b) \), then there exists at least one \( c \in (a, b) \) such that:
\( f(c) = N \)
⚠️ Requires CONTINUITY on the closed interval. Does not give the exact value of \( c \).

6. Asymptotes & End Behavior

Limits
Horizontal Asymptote \( y = L \):
\( \lim_{x \to \infty} f(x) = L \quad \text{or} \quad \lim_{x \to -\infty} f(x) = L \)
Vertical Asymptote \( x = c \):
\( \lim_{x \to c^\pm} f(x) = \pm\infty \) (denominator \( \to 0 \) while numerator \( \neq 0 \))
