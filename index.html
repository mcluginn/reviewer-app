1. Thermodynamics: State & Ideal Gas

GEN 0161
  • Ideal Gas Law: \( PV = m R T \quad (T \text{ in K}) \)
  • Quality \( x \): \( v = v_f + x v_{fg}, \quad x = \frac{m_g}{m_t} \)
  • First Law (Closed): \( \Delta U = Q - W \implies Q = \Delta U + W \)

2. Thermodynamics: Boundary Work

\( W_b = \int P dV \)
  • Isobaric (\( P=C \)): \( W_b = P(V_2 - V_1) \)
  • Isothermal (\( T=C \)): \( W_b = P_1 V_1 \ln(V_2 / V_1) \)
  • Polytropic (\( PV^n=C \)): \( W_b = \frac{P_2 V_2 - P_1 V_1}{1 - n} \)

3. Special Topics: Linear Algebra

BSIE 3219
  • 2x2 Determinant: \( \det(A) = ad - bc \)
  • Matrix Inverse: \( A^{-1} = \frac{1}{ad-bc}\begin{pmatrix}d & -b \\ -c & a\end{pmatrix} \)
  • Matrix Trace: \( \text{tr}(A) = a + d \)

4. Special Topics: Vectors & Counting

Plane & Series
  • Vector Dot Product: \( \mathbf{A} \cdot \mathbf{B} = A_x B_x + A_y B_y \)
  • Infinite GP Sum (\(|r|<1\)): \( S_\infty = \frac{a_1}{1 - r} \)
  • Combinations vs Perm: \( C(n, r) = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}, \; P = \frac{n!}{(n-r)!} \)
