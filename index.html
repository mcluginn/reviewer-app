High Yield Engineering Math Cheat Sheet

5-Minute Rapid Review

The essential algebraic identities, matrix operations, complex formulas, and combinatorics rules.

1. Significant Figures & Algebra

Foundations
  • Leading Zeros: Never count (0.005 -> 1 sig fig)
  • Trailing Zeros (with decimal): Always count (0.0520 -> 3 sig figs)
  • Difference of Squares: \( x^2 - a^2 = (x - a)(x + a) \)

2. 2x2 Matrix Operations

Linear Algebra
Determinant: \( \det(A) = ad - bc \)
Trace: \( \text{tr}(A) = a + d \)
Inverse: \( A^{-1} = \frac{1}{\det(A)} \begin{pmatrix} d & -b \\ -c & a \end{pmatrix} \)
Singular Matrix: \( \det(A) = 0 \implies \text{No inverse} \)

3. Complex Numbers & Vectors

Geometry & Plane
Imaginary Unit: \( i = \sqrt{-1}, \quad i^2 = -1, \quad i^4 = 1 \)
Vector Magnitude: \( |a\mathbf{i} + b\mathbf{j}| = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2} \)
Dot Product: \( \mathbf{A} \cdot \mathbf{B} = A_x B_x + A_y B_y \)
Perpendicular: \( \mathbf{A} \cdot \mathbf{B} = 0 \iff \theta = 90^\circ \)

4. Progressions & Infinite Series

AP & GP
Arithmetic Progression (AP):
\( a_n = a_1 + (n - 1)d, \quad S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a_1 + a_n) \)
Geometric Progression (GP):
\( a_n = a_1 r^{n-1}, \quad S_n = \frac{a_1(1-r^n)}{1-r} \)
Infinite GP Sum (\( |r| < 1 \)):
\( S_\infty = \frac{a_1}{1 - r} \)

5. Permutations & Combinations

Counting
Linear Arrangement: \( P(n, r) = \frac{n!}{(n-r)!}, \quad P(n, n) = n! \)
Circular Arrangement: \( \text{Ways} = (n - 1)! \)
Unordered Selection: \( C(n, r) = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!} \)
Polygon Diagonals: \( D = \frac{n(n - 3)}{2} \)

6. Binomial Theorem & Means

Expansions
Binomial Term Count:
\( (a + b)^n \implies n + 1 \text{ terms} \)
Means Inequality:
\( \text{AM} = \frac{a+b}{2} \ge \text{GM} = \sqrt{ab} \ge \text{HM} = \frac{2ab}{a+b} \)
